About This Product

Our Natural Tapioca Litter is made from premium tapioca starch and corn starch, creating a powerful yet gentle litter solution. The unique non-stick bottom formula prevents litter from adhering to your cat's litter box, making cleanup easier than ever.

With 99% biodegradability, this litter breaks down naturally, reducing environmental impact. The fast-clumping action locks in moisture and odors, while the natural formula is gentle on sensitive paws.

Ingredients

Tapioca Starch: Natural starch from cassava roots
Corn Starch: Plant-based binding agent
Natural Minerals: For enhanced odor control
