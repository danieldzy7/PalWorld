# Story Page Image Setup

## Image Folder Structure

To display your cat photos on the "Our Story" page, please create the following folder and add your images:

### Required Folder:
```
img/story/
```

### Required Images:

1. **story (1).png** - Your five cats together (for "It All Started With Five Cats" section)
   - Recommended: A group photo or collage showing all five cats
   - Size: 800x600px or similar (landscape orientation works best)

2. **story (2).png** - Testing different litters (for "The Search That Never Ended" section)
   - Recommended: Photo showing different litter products or your search process
   - Size: 800x600px or similar

3. **story (3).png** - Cats during gaming sessions (for "Our Constant Companions" section)
   - Recommended: Pixel on your desk during gaming, or cats around your gaming setup
   - Size: 800x600px or similar

4. **story (4).png** - Developing the product (for "Creating Something Better" section)
   - Recommended: Photos of your cats testing the litter, or product development
   - Size: 800x600px or similar

5. **story (5).png** - Happy cats today (for "Sharing the Love" section)
   - Recommended: Current photos of your happy, healthy cats
   - Size: 800x600px or similar

## Steps to Add Your Images:

1. Create a folder: `img/story/`
2. Name your cat photos exactly as shown above (with the parentheses and .png extension)
3. Refresh the story page to see your images!

## Image Tips:

- Use high-quality photos (at least 800px wide)
- Images can be .png or .jpg format (just rename to .png)
- Landscape orientation (horizontal) photos work best
- Images will be automatically rounded and styled
