About This Product

Our Multi-Cat Formula is specifically engineered for households with multiple cats. With 3X strength and 50% absorbency, this powerful litter handles heavy use while maintaining exceptional odor control at 99.5% effectiveness.

The heavy-duty formula features fast multi-clumping technology that quickly isolates waste from multiple cats, keeping the litter box fresh throughout the day. Extended freshness means less frequent complete changes, saving you time and money.
